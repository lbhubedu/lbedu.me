## Our mission

**LBEdu** is a social initiative founded by Felipe Leone in São Paulo, Brazil, to bring technology closer to people with fewer opportunities to access it. The project combines computer reuse with digital education and accessible, responsible use of artificial intelligence.

The initiative is run by an individual and is under development. Its purpose is to create opportunities for more people to learn, build independence and explore what technology can make possible.

## Turning access into opportunity

- **Restoration and donation:** receive computers and components, assess their condition and prepare them for a new use whenever possible.
- **Digital inclusion:** bring computers and knowledge closer to low-income people and people with disabilities.
- **Technology and AI education:** encourage free learning in plain language, with attention to privacy, security and the limits of artificial intelligence.
- **Partnerships:** connect people and businesses willing to donate equipment, share knowledge or support the initiative.

The availability of equipment, classes and activities depends on the project's capacity and partnerships at any given time. Contact us to learn about current opportunities.

## Related projects

LBEdu also brings together ideas and tools designed to make technology easier to understand and more accessible:

- **LB Learn** — a project intended to explain repositories, applications and technology concepts in plain language. Its repository is currently private.
- **[LB do Linux](https://lb-linux.vercel.app)** — a Portuguese-language guide to discovering and learning to use Linux distributions, including on older computers. Its source repository is currently private.
- **[LB Design](https://github.com/lbhubedu/lb-design)** — a Portuguese-language library of design references and prompts for creating interfaces and digital projects.

Each project has its own development stage, availability and license. Check its documentation for current conditions.

## Sponsor

**[Papelaria Piti](https://www.piti.com.br/)** sponsors LBEdu. Founded in 1993, Piti supplies school and office materials to schools, families and businesses.

## Get involved

You can contribute equipment, knowledge, time or partnerships. To discuss donations, classes, volunteering or activities for businesses, visit [lbedu.me](https://lbedu.me) or write to [lb@lbedu.me](mailto:lb@lbedu.me).

Before donating a computer, back up important files and remove personal data if possible. If you need guidance, contact us before handing over the equipment.

### Donations via Pix

To support LBEdu financially, use the Pix key: **lb@lbedu.me**. Pix is a Brazilian payment system.
